I am a researcher in deep learning and computer vision. At [Antiplagiat](https://antiplagiat.ru/) I build the image-search stack behind plagiarism detection: image retrieval and matching, contrastive learning, detection of machine-generated images, a VLM-based model for evidence-based image matching, and handwritten text recognition (HTR/OCR).

The same toolbox goes into neuroscience: I decode visual experience from fMRI and EEG, from linear per-voxel forecasting to multimodal fMRI–EEG image reconstruction with CLIP alignment and diffusion models.

I hold BSc and MSc degrees from MIPT and started a PhD in AI and Machine Learning there in 2026, advised by [Andrey Grabovoy](https://andriygav.github.io/). Before Antiplagiat I worked on decentralized optimization at the MIPT Laboratory of Mathematical Methods of Optimization. I also teach: the Deep Learning course (computer vision, segmentation, image matching, VLMs) and the "My First Scientific Paper" seminar at MIPT.
